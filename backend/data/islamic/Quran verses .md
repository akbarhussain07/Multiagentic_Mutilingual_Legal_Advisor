# Islamic Laws in Quran Verses
## 1: Family Laws
### 1. Marriage (Nikah)
Quran 30:21
Sahih International:  
And one of His signs is that He created for you from yourselves spouses that you may find 
comfort in them, and He placed between you affection and mercy. Indeed, in that are signs 
for people who reflect.

Pickthall:   
And one of His signs is that He created for you mates from among yourselves that ye may 
find peace of mind in them, and He ordained between you love and mercy. Lo, herein 
indeed are portents for folk who reflect.

Context: This verse is often cited as the most beautiful description of marriage in the 
Quran. It emphasizes the divine wisdom behind marriage and the emotional and spiritual 
bond between spouses.

Quran 5:1
Sahih International:   
O you who have believed, fulfill [all] contracts. Indeed, lawful for you are the animals of 
grazing livestock except what is recited to you. But do not eat from that which has been 
slaughtered to other than Allah. Indeed, it is sin. And indeed, Satan does wish to lead you 
into clear error by that which has been forbidden to you. But if you err and then repent 
afterward and believe, then indeed, your Lord is Forgiving and Merciful.

Context: This verse speaks about fulfilling contracts, which includes the marriage contract 
(Nikah). It emphasizes the importance of honoring commitments in marriage.

Quran 24:33
Sahih International:   
And those who cannot find marriage due to poverty, let them be patient until Allah enriches 
them from His bounty. And those whose relatives are taken away from them and who are in 
need of support – then give them their due from the wealth of Allah which He has given you. 
And spend not corruptively from that which you do not know [its value].

Pickthall:   
And as for those who cannot find marriage, let them be patient until Allah gives them 
means by His grace. And such of your servants as have no property, ask help from Allah. If 
ye know any virtue in them, give them help therefrom. And give them of the wealth of Allah 
which He hath given you, and force not your slave girls to prostitution when they desire 
marriage, if they desire chastity, in order that ye may make a gain in the goods of this life. 
But if anyone compel them, then lo! after such compulsion Allah is Forgiving, Merciful.

Context: Encourages marriage and provides guidance for those who cannot afford it. It also 
speaks about the rights of the poor and needy in society.

Quran 4:22
Sahih International:   
And do not marry former wives of your fathers – except what has already occurred [in the 
past]. Indeed, it is ever immorality and hateful [and] and evil as a way.

Pickthall:   
And do not marry the women whom your fathers married (except what has already passed); 
lo! it is an abomination, and hateful, and an evil way.

Context: This verse outlines prohibitions in marriage, specifically forbidding marriage with 
the wives of one’s father (except in cases already passed).

Quran 5:5
Sahih International:   
This day [all] the good things have been made lawful for you. And the food of those who 
were given the Scripture is lawful for you, and your food is lawful for them. And [lawful in 
marriage are] chaste women from among the believers and chaste women from among 
those who were given the Scripture before you, when you have given them their due 
compensation, seeking married life, not committing adultery or taking [secret] lovers. And 
whoever denies the faith – then he has wasted his deeds, and indeed, he will be among the 
losers in the Hereafter.

Pickthall:   
This day are (all) good things made lawful for you. The food of those who have received the 
Scripture is lawful for you and your food is lawful for them. And (lawful are) chaste women 
from among the believers and chaste women from among those who received the Scripture 

before you (when ye give them their due) for marriage, not for fornication or secret 
friendship.

Context: This verse permits marriage with chaste women from among believers and People 
of the Book (Christians and Jews), provided the Mahr (dowry) is given and the marriage is 
conducted for the purpose of lawful union.

### 2. Divorce (Talaq)
Quran 2:228
Sahih International:   
Divorced women remain in a waiting period (‘Iddah) of three menstrual periods, and it is 
not lawful for them to conceal what Allah has created in their wombs if they believe in Allah 
and in the Last Day. And during this [waiting] period their husbands have the right to take 
them back if they desire reconciliation. And the wives have rights similar to those [rights] 
over their husbands according to what is reasonable, but men have a degree more than 
them. And Allah is Almighty, Wise.

Pickthall:   
Divorced women wait for three periods, and he whose wives divorce them have no right to 
them again until they have married another besides him. And if they return to him after that, 
then it is no sin for either of the two to reunite again, if they think that they can keep the 
limits of Allah, the Lord of Glory.

Context: This is one of the most important verses on divorce. It establishes the waiting 
period (‘Iddah’) of three menstrual cycles for divorced women before the divorce is 
finalized or reconciliation occurs. It also emphasizes the rights of both spouses.

Quran 2:231
Sahih International:   
And when you have divorced women and they have fulfilled their waiting period, either 
retain them according to reasonable terms or release them according to reasonable terms. 
But do not retain them to harm them or to take advantage of them; whoever does so is 
indeed committing a sin. And do not take Allah’s verses in mockery. And remember what 
has been revealed to you in the scriptures – of the signs of Allah and His wisdom – and be 
mindful of Allah. Indeed, Allah is Acquainted with what you do.

Pickthall:   
And when ye have divorced women, and they have fulfilled their term, do not prevent them 
from marrying their (former) husbands if they have agreed together in fair manner. This is an 
admonition for him among you who believe in Allah and the Last Day. That is for you, 
cleaner and purer. And Allah Koeth and Ye know not.

Context: Guidance on what to do after the ‘Iddah’ is over – either reconcile or release the 
woman with dignity. It forbids using divorce as a means of oppression.

Quran 65:1
Sahih International:   
O Prophet, when you [men] divorce women and have fulfilled from them their waiting 
period, either retain them according to reasonable terms or release them according to 
reasonable terms. And witness two men among you to this [act]. And the testimony of one 
man will not be accepted unless it is the testimony of two men.

Pickthall:   
O Prophet! When ye do divorce women, divorce them for their appointed period and count 
the period, and fear Allah, your Lord. Expel not them from their houses wherein they lived, 
nor let them go forth except they commit an open indecency. These are the limits of Allah. 
And whoso transgressed the limits of Allah, verily he hath done wrong unto himself.

Context: This verse outlines the process of divorce, emphasizing the need for witnesses 
and clarity in the waiting period. It also discusses the rights of women to their homes 
during and after divorce.

Quran 65:6
Sahih International:   
Lodge them [divorced women] where you live, according to what is customary, and do not 
cause them terrible harm and provide for them – one who is rich according to his means, 
and one who is poor according to his means – a provision according to what is reasonable. 
That is a duty upon the righteous.

Pickthall:   
Lodge them (in houses) where ye dwelt with them according to your means and harass 
them not so as to make life difficult for them. And if they are pregnant, spend it on them 
until they bring forth their burden. And if they give suck for thee, give them their wage 
graciously, and divide (the matter) between you and them in good conscience; but if ye 
make difficulty for one another, then some other woman may give suck for him.

Context: This verse emphasizes the responsibility of the husband to provide for divorced 
women, including lodging and maintenance, during the ‘Iddah’.

Quran 2:232
Sahih International:   
And if they [the divorced women] are pregnant, then spend on them until they give birth. 
And if they breastfeed for you, then give them their compensation and consult together in 
good conscience. But if you make difficulties for one another, then there is another woman 
who can breastfeed for you.

Pickthall:   
And if they are pregnant, spend on them till they lay their burden. And if they give suck for 
thee, give them their wage rightfully. And if ye make difficulty for one another regarding this, 
then let some other nurse him.

Context: Guidance on maintenance and breastfeeding after divorce if the child is born after 
divorce.

## 2. Muamalat (Transaction Laws)
### 1. Buying and Selling (Trade)
Quran 2:275
Sahih International:   
Those who consume interest cannot stand [on the Day of Resurrection] except as one 
stands who is being beaten by Satan into insanity. That is because they say, "Trade is [just] 
like interest." But Allah has permitted trade and has forbidden interest. So whoever has 
received an admonition from his Lord and desists may have what is past, and his affair 
rests with Allah. But whoever returns [to dealing in interest] – those are the companions of 
the Fire; they will abide therein eternally.

Context: This is the primary verse that distinguishes lawful trade from unlawful interest. It 
clearly states that trade is permitted by Allah, while interest is forbidden.

Quran 4:29
Sahih International:   
O you who have believed, do not consume one another's wealth unjustly but only [in 
lawful] business by mutual consent. And do not kill yourselves [or one another]. Indeed, 
Allah is to you ever Merciful.

Context: This verse establishes a foundational principle for all financial dealings: wealth 
must be earned through mutually consented, lawful trade. It prohibits fraud, exploitation, 
and any unjust consumption of property.

Quran 83:1-3
Sahih International:   
Woe to those who give less [than due],   
Who, when they take a measure from people, take in full,   
But when they give by measure or by weight to them, they give less than due.

Context: A powerful condemnation of fraud in trade, specifically cheating in weights and 
measures. It highlights the importance of honesty and fairness in all commercial 
transactions.

### 2. Riba (Usury/Interest) - Prohibited
Quran 2:275-279 (Continuation of the above)
Sahih International (2:275):   
...Allah has permitted trade and has forbidden interest...
(2:276): Allah destroys interest and gives increase for charities. And Allah does not like 
every sinning disbeliever.
(2:278-279): O you who have believed, fear Allah and give up what remains [due to you] of 
interest, if you should be believers. And if you do not, then be informed of a war [against 
you] from Allah and His Messenger. But if you repent, you may have your principal - [thus] 
you do no wrong, nor are you wronged.

Context: These are among the strongest verses against Riba. They declare war from Allah 
and His Messenger upon those who engage in it, while promising blessing for charity. It 
mandates the abandonment of all outstanding interest and allows only for the recovery of 
the principal amount.

Quran 3:130
Sahih International:   
O you who have believed, do not consume interest, multiplied many times over, but fear 
Allah that you may be successful.

## Contracts and Agreements
### Quran 5:1
Sahih International:   
O you who have believed, fulfill [all] contracts. Lawful for you are the animals of grazing 
livestock except what is recited to you [of the Quran] - hunting being unlawful when you are 
in the state of ihram. Indeed, Allah ordains what He intends.

Context: This is the key verse on fulfilling contracts. It commands believers to honor all 
binding agreements and promises, which includes business contracts, marriage contracts, 
treaties, and personal pledges.

### Quran 17:34
Sahih International:   
And do not approach the property of an orphan, except in the way that is best, until he 
reaches maturity. And fulfill [every] commitment. Indeed, the commitment is ever [that 
about which one will be] questioned.

Context: Emphasizes that every commitment or promise is a serious responsibility for 
which one will be held accountable.

### Quran 2:282 (The Verse of Debt)
Sahih International (Excerpt):   
O you who have believed, when you contract a debt for a specified term, write it down... 
And do not be averse to writing it, whether it is small or large, for its [specified] term. That is 
more just in the sight of Allah and more sound for evidence and more likely to prevent 
doubt between you... And let him who incurs the liability dictate... And get two witnesses 
from among your men... That is more just in the sight of Allah and stronger as evidence and 
more likely to prevent doubt between you, except when it is an immediate transaction 
which you conduct among yourselves...

Context: This is the longest verse in the Quran. It provides detailed divine guidance on 
documenting financial contracts and debts. It mandates written contracts with witnesses 
to ensure justice, clarity, and the prevention of future disputes. It is a cornerstone of 
Islamic commercial law.

### Quran 16:91
Sahih International:   
And fulfill the covenant of Allah when you have taken it, and do not break oaths after their 
confirmation while you have made Allah your surety. Indeed, Allah knows what you do.

Context: Commands the fulfilling of covenants made with Allah and with people, and 
strongly forbids breaking solemn oaths.

## Mawarith (Inheritance Laws)
### Core Inheritance Passage
#### Surah An‑Nisa (4:11–12, 4:176)
These are the primary inheritance verses.

##### a) Share of Children & Parents – 4:11
Quran 4:11   
Allah instructs you concerning your children:   
for the male, what is equal to the share of two females.   
But if there are only daughters, two or more, for them is two-thirds of what he left.   
And if there is only one, for her is half.   
And for one’s parents, to each one of them is a sixth of what he left if he had a child.   
But if he had no child and the parents [alone] inherit from him, then for his mother is one-
third.   
And if he had brothers [or sisters], for his mother is a sixth,   
after any bequest he [may have] made or debt.   
Your parents or your children – you do not know which of them are nearest to you in 
benefit.   
[These shares are] an obligation [imposed] by Allah. Indeed, Allah is ever Knowing and 
Wise.

This verse covers:
- Sons / daughters’ shares
- Parents’ shares
- Mentions effect of siblings on the mother’s share
- Mentions bequest (Wasiyyah) and debt to be paid first

##### b) Share of Spouses – 4:12
Quran 4:12   
And for you is half of what your wives leave if they have no child.   
But if they have a child, for you is one-fourth of what they leave,   
after any bequest they [may have] made or debt.   
And for them [your wives] is one-fourth of what you leave if you have no child.   
But if you have a child, then for them is one-eighth of what you leave,   
after any bequest you [may have] made or debt.   
And if a man or woman leaves neither ascendants nor descendants but has a [maternal] 
brother or a [maternal] sister, then for each one of them is a sixth.   
But if they are more than two, they share a third,   
after any bequest which was made or debt, as long as there is no detriment [caused].   
[This is] an ordinance from Allah, and Allah is Knowing and Forbearing.

This verse covers:
- Husband’s share
- Wife’s share
- Maternal half‑siblings’ shares (no parents/children: each 1/6; if more, together 1/3)
- Again: Wasiyyah and debts are executed first

##### c) Share of Siblings (Full / Paternal) – 4:176
Quran 4:176   
They request from you a [legal] ruling. Say, “Allah gives you a ruling concerning one having 
neither descendants nor ascendants [kalālah].   
If a man dies, leaving no child but [only] a sister, she will have half of what he left.   
And he inherits from her if she [dies and] has no child.   
But if there are two sisters, they will have two-thirds of what he left.   
If there are brothers and sisters, the male will have the share of two females.   
Allah makes clear to you [His law], lest you go astray. And Allah is Knowing of all things.”

This verse covers:
- Full / paternal siblings’ shares in case of kalālah (no parents or children)
- Male sibling = share of two females

## By Your Sub‑Topics
### A) Share of Sons / Daughters
Main verse: 4:11

Quran 4:11 (part)   
Allah instructs you concerning your children:   
for the male, what is equal to the share of two females.   
But if there are only daughters, two or more, for them is two-thirds of what he left.   
And if there is only one, for her is half.

Summary from this verse:
- Son & daughter together:   
  - Male = 2 shares, Female = 1 share
- Only daughters: 
  - 2 or more daughters: 2/3 of the estate 
  - 1 daughter: 1/2 of the estate

### B) Share of Parents
Main verse: 4:11

Quran 4:11 (part)   
And for one’s parents, to each one of them is a sixth of what he left if he had a child.   
But if he had no child and the parents [alone] inherit from him, then for his mother is one-
third.   
And if he had brothers [or sisters], for his mother is a sixth,   
after any bequest he [may have] made or debt…

Key points:
- If the deceased has children: 
  - Father: 1/6   
  - Mother: 1/6 
- If no children and only parents: 
  - Mother: 1/3   
  - (Father takes the rest after fixed shares)
- If no children but deceased has siblings: 
  - Mother: 1/6   
  - (Siblings may not always inherit directly; this affects the mother’s portion.)

### C) Share of Spouse (Husband / Wife)
Main verse: 4:12

Quran 4:12 (part)   
And for you is half of what your wives leave if they have no child.   
But if they have a child, for you is one-fourth of what they leave…   
And for them [your wives] is one-fourth of what you leave if you have no child.   
But if you have a child, then for them is one-eighth of what you leave…

Key points:
- Husband inherits from wife: 
  - If she has no children: husband gets 1/2 
  - If she has children: husband gets 1/4 
- Wife inherits from husband (one or more wives share this): 
  - If he has no children: wife/wives get 1/4 (shared) 
  - If he has children: wife/wives get 1/8 (shared)

### D) Share of Siblings
#### 1) Maternal half‑siblings (no parents, no children) – 4:12 (end)
Quran 4:12 (end)   
And if a man or woman leaves neither ascendants nor descendants but has a [maternal] 
brother or a [maternal] sister, then for each one of them is a sixth.   
But if they are more than two, they share a third, after any bequest which was made or 
debt…

- One maternal brother or sister: 1/6 
- Two or more maternal siblings: together 1/3, divided equally

#### 2) Full / paternal siblings – 4:176
Quran 4:176

And he inherits from her if she [dies and] has no child.   
But if there are two sisters, they will have two-thirds of what he left.

# Main Title
## Introduction to Islamic Laws
### General Bequest Instruction
3) 2:180 – General bequest instruction 
Quran 2:180   
Prescribed for you, when death approaches any one of you, if he leaves wealth, is that he 
should make a bequest for the parents and near relatives according to what is acceptable – 
a duty upon the righteous. 
 
(This early verse is understood together with the detailed fixed-shares of Surah An‑Nisa; 
classical fiqh explains that wasiyyah cannot override the fixed shares, and cannot be to 
those who are already fixed heirs.)

## Jinayat (Criminal Laws/Hudud)
### List of Crimes
1. Theft (Sariqah)  Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:38 
 
 “[As for] the thief, the male and the female, amputate their hands in recompense for what 
they committed as a deterrent [punishment] from Allah. And Allah is Exalted in Might and 
Wise.”   
 (Quran 5:38) 
 
2. Adultery (Zina) – Surah An-Nur 24:2 
 
 “The [unmarried] woman or [unmarried] man found guilty of sexual intercourse – lash each 
one of them with a hundred lashes, and do not be taken by pity for them in the religion of 
Allah, if you should believe in Allah and the Last Day. And let a group of the believers 
witness their punishment.”   

 (Quran 24:2) 
 
3. False Accusation (Qadhf) – Surah An-Nur 24:4 
 
 “And those who accuse chaste women and then do not produce four witnesses – lash 
them with eighty lashes and do not accept from them testimony ever after. And those are 
the defiantly disobedient.”   
 (Quran 24:4) 
 
4. Highway Robbery / Waging War / Terror (Hirabah) – Surah Al-Ma'idah 
5:33 
 
 “Indeed, the penalty for those who wage war against Allah and His Messenger and strive 
upon earth [to cause] corruption is none but that they be killed or crucified or that their 
hands and feet be cut off on opposite sides or that they be exiled from the land. That is for 
them a disgrace in this world; and for them in the Hereafter is a great punishment.”   
 (Quran 5:33) 
 
5. Murder / Legal Retaliation (Qisas) – Surah Al-Baqarah 2:178 
 
 “O you who have believed, prescribed for you is legal retribution for those murdered – the 
free for the free, the slave for the slave, and the female for the female. But whoever 

overlooks from his brother anything, then there should be a suitable follow-up and 
payment to him with good conduct. This is an alleviation from your Lord and a mercy. But 
whoever transgresses after that will have a painful punishment.”   
 (Quran 2:178) 
 
6. Alcohol / Intoxicants – Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:90 
 
 “O you who have believed, indeed, intoxicants, gambling, [sacrificing on] stone altars [to 
other than Allah], and divining arrows are but defilement from the work of Satan, so avoid it 
that you may be successful.”   
 (Quran 5:90) 

## Dietary Laws (Haram Foods)
### List of Prohibited Foods
1. Pork   
 
Quran 2:173   
 Sahih International:   

 O you who have believed, lawful to you are all good foods, but do not approach dead 
animals, blood, the flesh of swine, and meat over which the name of other than Allah has 
been mentioned, or one that has been strangled, or beaten to death, or a fallen animal, or 
one gored. And [also prohibited is] an animal sacrificed on a stone altar or one divided [by 
idols] by divination. That is grave disobedience. This is so you may attain taqwa (piety).   
 
Quran 5:3   
 Sahih International:   
 This day have I perfected for you your religion and completed My favor upon you and have 
approved for you Islam as your religion. But whoever is forced by severe hunger, with no 
intention of sinning – indeed, Allah is Forgiving and Merciful.   
 
 Context: The verse seals the prohibition of impurities, including pork, as part of the 
completed Islamic legal code.   

2. Blood   
 
Quran 2:173 (as above)   
 ...and blood... is grave disobedience.   
 
Quran 5:4   
 Sahih International:   
 [Allah says]: “Eat not blood; indeed, it is filth.”   
 
 Context: Blood (whether drained from animals or consumed in any form) is explicitly 
forbidden as ritually impure.   

3. Dead Animals (Carrion)   
 
Quran 2:173 (as above)   
 ...and dead animals... is grave disobedience.   
 
Quran 6:145   
 Sahih International:   
 Say, [O Muhammad], I do not find within that which has been revealed to me anything 
unlawful to one who eats of it, except a dead animal, or blood spilled, or the flesh of swine 
– or flesh which has been deviled with by being dedicated to other than Allah. But [if one] is 
forced by severe hunger, with no intention to commit disobedience – indeed, Allah is 
Forgiving and Merciful.   
 
 Context: “Dead animals” (maytah) refer to animals that died a natural death (not 
slaughtered properly). Consuming them is prohibited unless under extreme necessity.   

4. Alcohol (Intoxicants)   
 
Quran 5:90   
 Sahih International:   
 O you who have believed, indeed, intoxicants, gambling, [sacrificing on] stone altars [to 
other than Allah], and divining arrows are but defilement from the work of Satan. So avoid it 
that you may be successful.   

 Satan only intends through them to provoke animosity and envy between you and to 
prevent you from remembering Allah and from prayer. Will you then cease?   
 
 Context: Alcohol and all intoxicants that cloud the mind are strictly prohibited as they lead 
to moral and spiritual corruption.   

5. Animals Not Slaughtered Properly   
 
This includes animals killed by strangling, bludgeoning, falls, goring, wild beasts, or 
sacrifices to other than Allah.   
 
Quran 2:173 (as above)   
 ...and meat over which the name of other than Allah has been mentioned, or one that has 
been strangled, or beaten to death, or a fallen animal, or one gored...   
 
Quran 6:121   
 Sahih International:   
 And do not eat from that upon which the name of Allah has not been mentioned, for 
indeed, it is an act of unfaithfulness. And indeed, the devils do whisper to their associates 
to argue with you, but if you should obey them – indeed, you would be polytheists.   
 
 Context: Islamic law requires animals to be slaughtered (Dhabihah) by a Muslim, 
Christian, or Jew, invoking Allah’s name (“Bismillah”). Meat not slaughtered this way (e.g., 
pork, meat from non-Muslims who do not believe in One God, or improperly killed animals) 
is forbidden.
